# Code of Conduct

Participants are expected to be respectful, precise, and safety-minded. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or expose another person. Security research must remain within an authorized, non-destructive scope.

Project maintainers may remove content or participation that violates this standard, risks secret or personal-data exposure, or attempts to bypass the project’s authority boundaries. Report conduct concerns privately through the security contact route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
